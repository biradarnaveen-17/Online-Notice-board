16 of 20

Open Innovation

On Campus

Participants should identify a local problem-such as traffic management, waste disposal, or access to education and build solutions using Google technologies to solve it or address pressing social issues around them.

​

Here are some few ideas…

  • Optimize city services like garbage collection or traffic flow.
  • Improve access to healthcare or education in underserved areas.
  • Promote sustainability, such as reducing energy consumption or encouraging recycling.
  • Enhance community engagement or safety through smart apps or platforms.

​

Participants are free to explore other pressing social issues in their communities and build solutions for them using Google technologies.

17 of 20

Google Technology

(Mandatory Requirement)

On Campus

Participants must integrate at least one Google technology in their solution.

​

This includes tools such as

  • Google Gemini
  • Google AI Studio
  • Firebase
  • Google Cloud Platform
  • Other relevant Google technologies.

​

Please ensure that participants clearly demonstrate how these technologies are incorporated into their solution.

Guidelines on Project Submission

Language Requirement: All submission materials, such as code, documentation, and presentations, must be submitted in English

Participants must submit the following as part of their project:

  • Project Deck/Presentation
  • Minimum Viable Product Link
  • Three-Minute Demo Video
  • GitHub Repository Link
  • List of Google Technologies Used(Mandatory use of one Google Tech)
  • List of Google AI Tools Integrated
  • Solution Description (100 Words)

Project created after 1st December, 2025 will be accepted for submission
